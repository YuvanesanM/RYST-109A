The Villa

Where the coast
is all yours

RYST 109A is a private beach villa on the ECR, tucked away on Pearl Beach in Parmankeni, Cheyyur — a pristine stretch of the Coromandel Coast about 85 km south of Chennai. It's a private villa in Tamil Nadu built for slow mornings and salt-air evenings.

The villa is exclusively yours for your entire stay. No shared lobbies, no strangers, no schedules — just you, your people, and the sea. Wake up to the sound of waves, take a short 500 m stroll to Pearl Beach, and watch the sun melt into the Bay of Bengal each evening.

It makes an ideal beach villa for family holidays, close-knit friend groups, or an easy weekend getaway from Chennai — for anyone who needs to truly get away.

The ECR coastline puts you a short drive from UNESCO heritage sites, backwaters and the French quarter of Pondicherry — day trips that fill a longer stay naturally.

Why RYST 109A

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

A luxury villa on the ECR, exclusively yours — private pool, jacuzzi and a short walk to the sea. The rare villa with a pool near Chennai where the whole beach house is yours alone.

Private Beach Access

A leisurely 500 m walk from the villa leads you to a quiet stretch of Pearl Beach — one of the Coromandel Coast's most unspoilt shores. Early morning walks, evening bonfires, the water entirely to yourself.

Exclusively Yours

The entire villa — comfortably hosting up to 16 guests — is yours alone. No shared spaces, no other guests, no interruptions. Absolute privacy from check-in to check-out.

Fully Furnished Kitchen

Cook your favourite meals with a fully equipped kitchen. Stock up at local markets and enjoy home-cooked food with a sea breeze — no dining schedule, no restaurant queue.

Jacuzzi & Pool

Unwind in the private jacuzzi or take a dip in the pool — day or night. Whether it's a sunrise soak or a moonlit float, it's entirely yours for the duration of your stay.

Outdoor Living & Games

Sea-facing terraces, open-air sit-outs, and a life-size chess set on the terrace. Watch the sunrise over the Bay, stargaze on clear nights, or let the sea breeze decide who blinks first over a chess match.

Game Room & Entertainment

A dedicated recreation hall with table tennis, foosball, darts, cornhole, board games and more — plus a JBL PartyBox speaker to keep the evening going. Rainy afternoon or late night after dinner, there's always a reason to stay in.

Easy Getaway

About 85 km south of Chennai via the ECR or GST Road — a pleasant drive that puts you in a completely different world in around two hours. No airports, no long journeys. When you want to venture out, the coast gives you beaches, forts and heritage sites all within a day trip.
