Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Your rights · Cookie policy

RYST 109A ("we", "us") is a private beach villa at Parmankeni, Cheyyur, Tamil Nadu, India, owned and run by its owner, Yuvanesan M. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit stay.ryst.in, message us, book or stay with us, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and, for guests who live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or elsewhere, the data protection laws that apply to you.

Who is responsible for your data (the "data fiduciary" / "controller")

Yuvanesan M, owner of RYST 109A
109A, Pearl Beach Site, Parmankeni, Cheyyur – 603305, Tamil Nadu, India

Email: info@ryst.in · WhatsApp: +91 81222 09109

Grievance Officer (India): Yuvanesan M, at the same address and email. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

We do not have an establishment or appointed representative in the EU or the UK; this website offers stays at a villa in India.

What we collect, and when

  • When you enquire or book on this website: your name, phone / WhatsApp number, email address, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests (adults and children), the occasion, any message you write, a coupon code if you use one, and the date and time you ticked the box agreeing to our terms and this policy.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp or Instagram: your number (or Instagram account name), your name as shown by the app, and your messages. Our assistant reads them to answer availability, prices and common questions (see Automated replies).
  • When you pay: the amount, date and Razorpay payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay directly; we never see or store them.
  • At online check-in: the lead guest's full name, the type of government ID, a photo of that ID, a signature, expected arrival time, vehicle number (if driving) and the number of guests. This is the guest register that Indian law requires us to keep (see Guest register).
  • During your stay: food orders from the guest portal, Wi-Fi voucher use, feedback and ratings you submit, and any issues you report.
  • Creators: if you apply on our creator page, your name, Instagram handle and follower count, phone, email, city, links to your work, your rate and your idea.
  • Website use: only if you agree in the cookie banner, Google Analytics (pages viewed, rough location from your IP, device type) and advertising measurement by Google Ads and Meta (whether an ad led to a booking). Without your agreement nothing of this runs. See the cookie policy.
  • Technical logs: our server keeps IP address, browser type and request times for up to 90 days, for security and to fix problems.

You can use the website without giving us any personal data. Name and phone number are needed to book; email is needed for an invoice. Children's names are not needed: we only ask how many guests are under 12 for pricing.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to you, quote, confirm and host your stay (performing the contract you ask for).
  • To meet legal duties: the guest register and ID checks required of accommodation providers in Tamil Nadu, reporting foreign nationals to the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (Form C), tax and accounting records, and lawful requests from authorities.
  • For our legitimate interests: keeping the villa and our guests safe, preventing fraud and misuse, following up on an enquiry you made, improving the villa and this website.
  • With your consent: analytics and advertising cookies, and promotional messages (offers, invitations to come back). You can withdraw consent at any time: change your cookie choice from "Cookie settings" in the footer, or reply STOP to any WhatsApp message. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Automated replies and AI

When you message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, an assistant run by our software may reply automatically with availability, prices, answers to common questions and, if you ask to book, a secure Razorpay payment link. A person reads every message, and anything the assistant cannot answer is handed to us. When our software cannot understand a message by itself, it may ask Anthropic's Claude model to read it (for example the dates and how many guests), and the automatic reply is then built from the villa's own calendar and prices. For some other replies our software prepares a draft with Claude from your message and the villa's own facts, and a person reviews the draft before it is sent. Only the message text is sent to the model, never your name or phone number.

WhatsApp, Instagram and email

We send messages about your booking (quote, payment link, check-in link, directions, reminders, invoice, a request for feedback after you leave) on WhatsApp through Interakt, a WhatsApp Business provider, or by email. These are part of the service you booked.

Promotional messages (for example an offer for open dates, or an invitation to return a year after your stay) are sent only to people who have enquired or stayed with us, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP; reply START to receive them again. Messages about a booking you hold still reach you after STOP.

WhatsApp and Instagram are run by Meta, whose own privacy policies apply to how they handle your messages.

Guest register, ID documents and the law

Accommodation providers in India must record the identity of every lead guest and show the register to the police on request; for foreign nationals we must also file Form C with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office within 24 hours of arrival. That is why online check-in asks for the lead guest's name, a government ID and a signature.

  • Any valid government photo ID is accepted. If you use Aadhaar, you may share a masked Aadhaar (only the last four digits visible) or a Virtual ID; we do not need or use your full Aadhaar number, and we never use Aadhaar authentication.
  • The ID photo is stored in private, access-controlled cloud storage and is seen only by the owner and the staff who handle check-in.
  • The ID photo is deleted automatically one year after check-out. The register entry itself (name, type of ID, dates, signature) is kept with the booking for as long as the law requires.
  • Foreign nationals: passport and visa details are shared with the FRRO through Form C, as the law requires.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that run the villa and this website, each for its purpose, and with authorities when the law requires:

  • Dreamour Multiventures (RYST Harbour), Chennai — provides and runs the software we use for bookings, messages, invoices and check-in, and processes data on our instructions (its privacy policy).
  • Supabase (database and private file storage), Render (server) and GitHub (website hosting and nightly backups).
  • Razorpay — payments and payment links.
  • Interakt and Meta — WhatsApp and Instagram messages.
  • Anthropic — reading messages the automatic reply cannot understand, and drafting some replies (your message text and the villa's facts; not your name or phone number).
  • Google — Google Analytics and Google Ads only if you accept those cookies; Google sign-in for our staff; Google Maps links.
  • Telegram — alerts to the owner and staff about bookings, payments, check-ins and messages (these can include your name, dates and number).
  • Open-Meteo — weather for the villa's location; no personal data is sent.
  • Online travel agencies (Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and others, if you booked through them) — only the dates, to keep calendars in sync.
  • Police, FRRO, tax and other authorities — when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of guests and the villa.

Some of these providers store data outside India, including in the United States and the European Union. If you are in the EU or UK, your data is transferred to India to perform your booking (Article 49(1)(b) GDPR) and to providers that use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and messages that do not lead to a booking: up to 12 months, then deleted; the message log keeps only the latest few hundred conversations.
  • Bookings, quotes, invoices and payment records: 8 years, as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Guest register: as long as the law requires. The ID photo: deleted one year after check-out.
  • Feedback and reviews: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Creator applications: 12 months after we decide, unless we work together.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days. Analytics data: as set out in the cookie policy.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves our backups as they are replaced, within 30 days.

How we protect it

All traffic uses HTTPS. Check-in and guest-portal links carry a signed token that only you receive. ID photos and signatures are in private storage that needs a signed link to open. Access is limited to the owner and the staff who need it, and each villa's data on the software we use is kept separate. Payment and messaging keys are encrypted. If a breach affects your data we will tell the Data Protection Board of India and you, as the law requires, and for EU/UK residents within 72 hours where the breach is likely to put your rights at risk.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, except what we must keep by law (for example invoices and the guest register);
  • withdraw consent for cookies or promotional messages;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or are unable to (India);
  • receive a copy of the data you gave us, restrict or object to some processing (EU and UK residents).

Email info@ryst.in or message us on WhatsApp saying what you need (for example "delete my data"). We will confirm your identity, reply within 30 days and tell you what was done. There is no charge.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to our Grievance Officer (above), and then to the Data Protection Board of India. EU residents may complain to their national data protection authority, and UK residents to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies unless you agree in the banner. What we use, why, how long it lasts and how to change your choice is in the cookie policy. The guest portal, check-in and booking pages keep a short session token in your browser so your booking opens; that is necessary and needs no consent. A guest's Instagram reel on our home page loads from Instagram only if you tap play.

Children

Bookings must be made by an adult (18 or over). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; we only ask how many children are in your group. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Links to Instagram, Google Maps, WhatsApp, Razorpay and online travel agencies take you to services with their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. For important changes we will tell guests with an upcoming booking by WhatsApp or email. The version in force when you booked applies to that booking, except where the law requires otherwise.
