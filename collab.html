Good to know

Creator questions

Is the stay complimentary?+

Every collaboration is different. Depending on your content plan and audience, we offer a complimentary or a discounted stay, and we confirm the terms with you on WhatsApp before you travel.

Do I need a minimum number of followers?+

No. We care more about the quality of your content and how engaged your audience is than about the follower count.

Which dates can I ask for?+

Weekday nights from Monday to Thursday that are still open, up to two nights, booked at least three days ahead. Weekends and holidays are kept for regular bookings.

Can I bring friends or family?+

Yes. The villa hosts up to 16 guests. Food and extras like the BBQ are charged separately unless we agree otherwise.

What do you expect after the stay?+

The content we agree on, posted within two weeks of your stay, tagging @stay.ryst with us invited as a collaborator on reels. We may reshare your content with credit.

How does the follower code work?+

Once approved, you get your own code for your audience. It works when they book online at stay.ryst.in, and we can see how many bookings it brings.

I'm a brand or agency, or I want a paid shoot+
